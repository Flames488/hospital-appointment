PRESENTATION MODE

Hospital workflow walkthrough

A guided patient-to-hospital demonstration for reviewing the workflow.

01 · PATIENT

Request an appointment

Open Patient booking, enter a fictional patient profile, choose a department and submit a request. Point out the generated reference and Pending status.

02 · STAFF

Review and confirm

Open Staff portal and sign in with the demo credentials. Search the patient, open View details and confirm the appointment. Show the status update.

03 · RECORDS

Show the patient profile

Open Patient records, search the patient and explain that their profile groups contact details and appointment history.

04 · MANAGEMENT

Explore reports and import

Show monthly and department charts, export a CSV, then demonstrate historical import using the supplied template.

Demonstration only: records are stored in this browser. Do not enter real patient information. Production use requires secure server-side storage, authentication, authorization, audit trails and privacy controls.
